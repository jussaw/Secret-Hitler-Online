# Sound credits

The game's sound effects live in `frontend/src/assets/sounds/`, one Ogg Opus and one MP3 file per
sound. Every clip is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0, so credit is a courtesy rather
than a requirement. A new clip must be CC0 too, and gets a row here.

Each clip was made from the high-quality preview on its Freesound page. The column "Part used"
gives the stretch of that preview, before trimming the silence at both ends, mixing to mono,
normalizing the loudness, and encoding as Ogg Opus (64 kbps) and MP3 (96 kbps).
